# CPD Portal - Complete Guide

## 📚 Overview

The CPD (Continuing Professional Development) Portal has three integrated sections:
1. **CPD Activities** - Log professional development hours
2. **Learning Plans** - Set and track development goals
3. **Peer Consultations** - Track consultation hours

## 🗓️ CPD Year Cycle

**Important:** CPD years run from **December 1 to November 30**

Example:
- **CPD Year 2025**: December 1, 2024 → November 30, 2025
- **CPD Year 2026**: December 1, 2025 → November 30, 2026

## 1️⃣ CPD Activities

### Features:
- **Log Activities**: Workshops, conferences, reading, online courses, etc.
- **Track Hours**: Progress toward 30-hour annual requirement
- **Three Views**: Weekly, Monthly, Yearly summaries
- **Link to Goals**: Connect activities to learning plan goals
- **PDF Export**: Download annual CPD report

### How to Use:
1. Click "**Add Activity**" button
2. Select activity type
3. Enter hours and description
4. Add reflection (optional)
5. **Link to goal** (optional - if you have an active learning plan)
6. Click "Save"

### Viewing Options:
- **Weekly**: See activities grouped by week with total hours
- **Monthly**: View monthly breakdown
- **All Activities**: Complete yearly list sorted by date

### Progress Tracking:
- Green progress bar shows completion (18/30 hours = 60%)
- Automatic calculation of remaining hours needed

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Plans

### Features:
- **Create Annual Plans**: One plan per CPD year
- **Add Goals**: Multiple learning goals with details
- **Track Progress**: See completed vs. active goals
- **Link Activities**: CPD activities and consultations appear under goals
- **Supervisor Comments**: Supervisors can add feedback
- **Mark as Finished**: Lock plan when year ends

### Creating a Learning Plan:

1. Navigate to "**Learning Plans**"
2. Click "**Create Learning Plan**"
3. Enter dates:
   - **Start Date**: December 1, 2024
   - **End Date**: November 30, 2025
4. Click "Create Plan"

### Adding Goals:

1. Click "**Add Goal**"
2. Fill in:
   - **Goal Title**: e.g., "Enhance trauma therapy skills"
   - **What I Want to Learn**: Detailed description
   - **Expected Outcomes**: What you hope to achieve
   - **Target Date**: Optional deadline
3. Click "Add Goal"

### Goal Details:
Each goal shows:
- Status badge (Active/Completed)
- What to learn
- Expected outcomes
- Target date
- **Linked items** (CPD activities & consultations related to this goal)

### Marking Goals Complete:
1. Expand goal in accordion
2. Click "**Mark as Completed**"
3. Goal gets strikethrough and "Completed" badge

### Finishing a Plan:
When the CPD year ends:
1. Click "**Mark as Finished**" button
2. Plan becomes read-only
3. Year stays in system for records
4. Create new plan for next year

---

## 3️⃣ Peer Consultations

### Features:
- **Log Consultations**: Track peer discussion sessions
- **Minutes Tracking**: Record time spent
- **Three Views**: Weekly, Monthly, All consultations
- **Link to Goals**: Connect consultations to learning objectives
- **Auto-calculate Hours**: Minutes automatically converted to hours

### Logging a Consultation:

1. Click "**Log Consultation**"
2. Enter:
   - **Date**: When consultation occurred
   - **Minutes Spent**: Duration (e.g., 60)
   - **Activity Description**: What was discussed
   - **Link to Goal**: Optional connection to learning plan
3. Click "Save Consultation"

### Summary Views:
- **Weekly**: Consultations grouped by week
- **Monthly**: Monthly totals
- **All**: Complete list for the year

### Hour Calculation:
- Displays total hours (e.g., "12.5h")
- Shows minutes (e.g., "750 minutes")
- Automatic conversion

---

## 🔗 Goal Linking Feature

### How It Works:
1. Create a learning plan with goals
2. When logging CPD activities or consultations, select a goal
3. Linked items automatically appear under that goal
4. View all related work in one place

### Benefits:
- **Organized tracking**: See all work toward each goal
- **Clear progress**: Visual evidence of development
- **Supervisor visibility**: Easy to show goal achievement

### Example:
**Goal**: "Enhance trauma therapy skills"

**Linked Items**:
- CPD Activity: "Trauma-Informed Practice Workshop" (6h)
- Peer Consultation: "Discussion on PTSD treatment approaches" (60m)
- CPD Activity: "Reading: Latest trauma therapy research" (4h)

**Total toward goal**: 11 hours

## 👨‍🏫 Supervisor Features

### Supervisor Access:
Supervisors can view **all three sections** for their psychologists:

1. **CPD Activities**: Read-only view of all activities
2. **Learning Plans**: 
   - View all goals
   - Add overall comments on the plan
   - See linked activities and consultations
3. **Peer Consultations**: View all consultation logs

### Adding Supervisor Comments:

1. Navigate to psychologist's Learning Plan
2. Click "**Add Comment**" in Supervisor Comments section
3. Enter feedback
4. Click "Add Comment"

Comments include:
- Supervisor name
- Date
- Feedback text
- Displayed in amber-colored boxes

## 📊 Year Management

### Creating a New Year:

When CPD year ends (Nov 30):
1. Click "**New Year**" button
2. System creates next CPD year
3. Previous year remains in system (archived)
4. Use year dropdown to switch between years

### Year Selection:
- Dropdown at top of each section
- Shows all available CPD years
- Each section filters data by selected year

## 💡 Best Practices

### For Psychologists:

1. **Log regularly**: Add activities soon after completion
2. **Create plan early**: Set goals at start of CPD year
3. **Link activities**: Connect work to specific goals
4. **Add reflections**: Document learning and growth
5. **Review progress**: Check dashboard regularly
6. **Mark goals complete**: Update status as you achieve goals
7. **Finish plan**: Mark as complete at year end

### For Supervisors:

1. **Review regularly**: Check psychologist progress monthly
2. **Add comments**: Provide feedback on goals and plans
3. **Monitor hours**: Ensure adequate CPD completion
4. **Guide development**: Suggest activities for unmet goals

## 🎯 Complete Workflow Example

**September 2024**: 
- Create Learning Plan for CPD Year 2025 (Dec 1, 2024 - Nov 30, 2025)
- Add Goal: "Develop DBT skills"

**December 2024**:
- Log CPD Activity: "DBT Workshop" (8h) → Link to DBT goal

**February 2025**:
- Log Peer Consultation: "DBT strategies discussion" (90m) → Link to DBT goal

**March 2025**:
- Log CPD Activity: "DBT Online Course" (6h) → Link to DBT goal
- Check goal progress: 15h toward DBT development
- Mark goal as completed

**November 2025**:
- Review all CPD activities: 30+ hours completed
- Supervisor adds comment: "Excellent progress on DBT skills"
- Mark Learning Plan as finished
- Export PDF reports

**December 2025**:
- Create new Learning Plan for CPD Year 2026
- Continue tracking in new year

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Q: Can't link activity to goal?**
A: Ensure you have an active learning plan with active goals

**Q: Don't see year dropdown?**
A: Create your first CPD year using "New Year" button

**Q: Supervisor can't comment?**
A: Only supervisors see comment button; psychologists view comments only

**Q: Want to edit a goal?**
A: Currently goals can be marked complete but not edited (contact admin for changes)

**Q: Year dates wrong?**
A: CPD years run Dec 1 - Nov 30 (not Jan 1 - Dec 31)
